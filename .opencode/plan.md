# Cane — Development Plan (summary)

The canonical development plan lives in the project [`README.md`](../README.md): [§2 System overview](../README.md#2-system-overview), [§3 Hardware](../README.md#3-hardware), [§4 Software](../README.md#4-software), [§5 Evaluation plan](../README.md#5-evaluation-plan), [§6 Development phases](../README.md#6-development-phases), [§7 Risks](../README.md#7-risks-and-limitations).

Status: P1 (architecture & component selection) **complete** — architecture as of 2026-09-08, component selection as of 2026-10-01. The board class is **decided: the wearable is a Raspberry Pi 5, 4 GB** (4 GB is ample for the resident workload); the vision tier's camera count is **1 or 2, adopted at the T7 placement gate** ([README §3](../README.md#3-hardware)). Selections, quantities, and PH-local costs: [`docs/hardware.md`](../docs/hardware.md) (pointer + wearable; board-agnostic rows vs the selected class rows; the priced esp32-s3-class flip path in §4). The complete cart: [`docs/purchase-list.md`](../docs/purchase-list.md) (≈ ₱24,300–41,800 before contingency). The bench-phase-only material slice: [`docs/bench.md`](../docs/bench.md) (≈ ₱1,600–3,500, conditional on the hardware bench path). Open lanes: the co-researcher's **purchase-approval sign-off** before checkout, then arrival. Purchasing constraints that bind the order: tracking hardware is mandatory (part of the final design of both devices), modules ordered connector-ready/pre-fitted, and each component category is bought once. A board-class flip is not bought up front — it routes through the priced esp32-s3 path ([`docs/hardware.md`](../docs/hardware.md) §4) under [README §3](../README.md#3-hardware) constraint 5. The plan carries no "frozen / core / locked" framing — everything stays open to change up to purchase; the baseline-commit evaluation protocol is retired (2026-10-01), and the change procedure (dependency chains, honest impact framing, write-only-on-explicit-instruction) is in [`instruction.md`](instruction.md). Marker scheme decided 2026-09-21 (reversing the 2026-09-15 two-LED beacon): a printed ArUco/AprilTag on the pointer shell, overwriting **position only** — the tag's PnP orientation is ignored (orientation stays on the pointer IMU over the device-to-device link); dictionary and tag size are pinned at T7, with a low-light/contrast check there ([§3.8](../README.md#38-pointer-tracking-hardware--notes--contingencies)). P2 (breadboard bench) is zero soldering; [`docs/bench-tests.md`](../docs/bench-tests.md) holds the P2 protocol with an automated logging pipeline added 2026-09-27 (event stream → capture → reduce → the mandated per-session CSV + acceptance check; the `bench-log` module joins README §4.1) — its wiring maps are drawn up once the compatible boards are selected. **Assembly & outsourcing (decided 2026-09-26, [README §3.9](../README.md#39-assembly--outsourcing-p3))**: the P3 soldered build of both devices is designed here and **soldered by an external local hand-solder service** — schematics, KiCad carrier boards (hand-solderable by design, through-hole-dominant, no fine-pitch SMT on our boards), harness/wiring design, and assembly drawings in-house; bare PCBs fab-ordered **with spares**; everything returning from the service passes in-house **incoming inspection before any power-up**, then the T0–T8 parity re-run; the one-time full soldering kit shrinks to a **minimal rework & inspection set** ([README §3.4](../README.md#34-assembly--bench-tools-one-time)); the operational protocol owns [`docs/assembly.md`](../docs/assembly.md); service quotes/booking land inside the P2 window and fold into the co-researcher's purchase-approval sign-off ([README §9](../README.md#9-open-items)); P3 window (Nov 2–21), exit (breadboard parity), and every defense date are unchanged ([`docs/schedule.md`](../docs/schedule.md) ladder item 7). Open items: the compatibility re-selection (esp32-s3 + raspi-5), purchase-approval sign-off, the assembly-service selection, and the PCB-fab order (see [§9 Open items](../README.md#9-open-items)).

Schedule (updated 2026-10-01): the combined development + thesis calendar ([`docs/schedule.md`](../docs/schedule.md)) is anchored on the two defenses — pre-oral Fri Dec 11, 2026 (full draft per completion status; the human study runs after it), final Tue Apr 20, 2027 (system and paper 100%). The component selection is complete (Raspberry Pi 5, 4 GB wearable, itemized — README §3/§9), so the ordering lane's only open gate is the purchase-approval sign-off: its working envelope in [`docs/schedule.md`](../docs/schedule.md) (sign-off ~Oct 8, arrival ~Oct 10–13) is absorbed by the pre-P3 buffer per contingency 1; the breadboard bench still runs to an **Oct 27** exit, and the soldered build Nov 2–21, calibration Nov 23–Dec 8, human study Jan 4 – Feb 26, 2027, system complete by Mar 31 — all unchanged.
