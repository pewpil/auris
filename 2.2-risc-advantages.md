# 2.2 Advantages (RISC)

- Simpler, easier-to-verify hardware design.
- Higher clock speeds and efficient pipelines.
- Lower power consumption.
- Compilers can schedule and optimize effectively.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    S[Simple ISA] --> HW[Simpler Hardware]
    HW --> CLK[Higher Clock Speed]
    HW --> PWR[Lower Power]
    S --> COMP[Compiler Friendly]
```
